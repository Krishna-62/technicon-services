## Brand & Style

The design system is engineered for high-density enterprise environments where clarity and authority are paramount. The brand personality is rooted in "Technocratic Elegance"—combining the precision of an ERP with a premium, executive aesthetic.

## Colors

The color palette is anchored by the "Deep Forest Green" primary (#063D2B). Background uses #F8FAF9 off-white.

## Typography

Utilizes Inter exclusively.

## Components

Buttons, Sidebar, Cards, Input Fields, Data Tables.
